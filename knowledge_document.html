Value Propositions by Cloud

Agentforce

Build, deploy, and supervise autonomous AI agents that take action across sales, service, and marketing using your Salesforce data, business logic, and guardrails. Reasoning runs on the Atlas Reasoning Engine and is grounded in Data Cloud so every agent answers with the same trusted context.

Sales Cloud

Close more deals faster with a CRM that automates pipeline hygiene, surfaces the next best action, and lets reps spend more time selling. Einstein forecasting and AI-generated outreach mean managers know where the gaps are before the quarter slips.

Marketing Cloud

Orchestrate personalized journeys across email, mobile, ads, and web with unified profiles from Data Cloud and generative content built right into the campaign canvas.

Ideal Customer Profile

Salesforce serves businesses from startups to the largest enterprises across automotive, financial services, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, and beyond. Primary buyers are sales leaders, customer service executives, marketing leaders, IT decision makers, and C-suite executives at B2B and B2C organizations that need a unified, AI-ready system of engagement.

Use Cases

  • Autonomous outbound prospecting and pipeline generation with Agentforce SDR.
  • Real-time case deflection and resolution with Agentforce Service.
  • Account-based marketing with unified profiles and AI-personalized journeys.
  • Forecasting, deal coaching, and territory planning across global sales orgs.
  • Unified self-service portals for customers, partners, and employees.
